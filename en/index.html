About

Products

Lumia: Menopause Tracking

Women's health · Mobile app

Lumia is a tracking and information app for women in perimenopause and menopause. It keeps your symptom, cycle, hormone/medication regimen and sleep records, lets you see how things are progressing, helps you keep your treatment on track and stands by you throughout the process.
